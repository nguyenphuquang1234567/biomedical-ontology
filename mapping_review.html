Example 1: grant funding

Actual source: stg_grant_funding.csv
appl_id: 2457869
award_key: P01AI039808
fiscal_year: 1997
award_amount: 780565

BEFORE: GAP — no financial amount or funding record.
AFTER:
GrantFundingRecord 2457869
    fiscalYear: 1997
    awardAmount: 780565.0
    fundingFor → GrantAward P01AI039808

Currency is unknown. No USD label or project total has been invented.

Example 2: principal investigator

Actual source: stg_grant_award.csv
contact_pi_name: BUCHMEIER, MICHAEL JOSEPH

BEFORE: GAP — no investigator role.
AFTER:
GrantAward P01AI039808
    contactPIMention → PersonNameMention
        label: BUCHMEIER, MICHAEL JOSEPH
        nameResolutionStatus: reported_only

This preserves the reported name and role; it does not establish a globally unique Person.

Example 3: patent inventor and dates

Actual source: patent_evidence.csv, PATENT_0001
inventor: Ahmed; Mahiuddin et al.
filing_date: 2020-08-19
date: 2021-12-14

BEFORE: GAP for inventor; PARTIAL for dates.
AFTER:
Patent US 11197910 B1
    inventorMention → PersonNameMention
        label: Ahmed; Mahiuddin et al.
        nameResolutionStatus: incomplete_list
    filingDate: 2020-08-19
    reportedDate: 2021-12-14
    reportedDateMeaning: unverified source date role

Do not assume the generic date means publication date. Do not invent missing inventor names.

RESOLVED means a faithful storage/relationship model now exists. It does not mean that source facts, currency, taxon namespace, date meaning, or identity have been independently verified. Search relevance is never converted into scientific support.

What has actually been generated?

687 award records, 2,426 funding records and associated abstract/document records, and 72 patent records have been mapped into local metadata subgraphs. The 2,426 funding-to-award links were checked. No hypotheses, scientific claims, or discoveries have been inferred from metadata.

Open ontology_explorer.html for the full class catalog, including inherited-attribute search. Full imported metadata graphs remain local and are not bundled in this repository. The ontology and mapping review are published; raw input CSVs are not included.
